How it works

Your team's full working day — in one app

From arriving on site to collecting the customer's signature, the Coglass app handles every step.

  1. See today's jobs

    Field staff open the app and see their assigned jobs for the day — customer name, address, visit type (survey, fitting, delivery) and time slot. All assigned by the office via the planning board.

  2. Check the job details

    Tap any job to see the full order: customer contact, site address, access notes, special instructions, previous survey photos and glass specifications. Everything they need before ringing the doorbell.

  3. Capture the survey

    Start a survey visit, measure each pane, add drawings and photos. All measurements are captured in millimetres with location labels, shape, operations and notes — synced to the office when back in signal.

  4. Record the fitting

    On a fitting visit, work through the completion checklist. Mark items as fitted, record snag notes and photos, capture GPS proof of location, and note any access issues or follow-up work needed.

  5. Collect signature

    Once work is complete, the customer signs directly on the screen. The signed record is stored against the job — time-stamped, GPS-tagged and available in the office immediately.

  6. Works offline

    No signal on site? The app keeps working. All data is captured and queued locally. The moment signal returns, everything syncs automatically — photos, measurements, signatures, all of it.

What's included

Built for glaziers who work on site every day

Not a watered-down version of the desktop — a fully featured field app designed for how glaziers actually work.

  • Daily jobs listSee all jobs assigned for today with customer, address, visit type and time — assigned by the office from the planning board.
  • Full job details on siteCustomer contact, access notes, special instructions, glass specs, previous surveys and photos — all visible before arriving on site.
  • Survey and measurement captureMeasure each pane, record shape, operations (Georgian bars, cutouts, lead) and photos — all synced to the order.
  • Fitting completion recordChecklist-based fitting record with snag notes, photos and partial fitting tracking with reasons.
  • Customer digital signatureCustomers sign on screen at completion. Signature stored time-stamped and GPS-tagged against the job.
  • GPS proof of locationLocation is recorded with arrival, departure and sign-off — proof the team was on site.
  • Offline modeKeeps working without signal. Changes are queued on the phone and sync when the connection returns.
  • Push notificationsStaff get a push notification when today's bookings change, when they are tagged in a job update, and for office alerts.
  • Shop-floor iPad appWorkshop staff run the shop-floor app on an iPad — cut lists by station, barcode scan to advance, and collection sign-off.
  • Photo captureTake photos on site and attach to measurements, surveys or job notes. Photos upload automatically when back in signal.
  • Planner viewSee your schedule for the coming days — upcoming jobs, visit types and time slots all visible in the planner tab.
  • iOS and AndroidAvailable on both platforms. Field teams can use their own devices — no specific hardware required.
