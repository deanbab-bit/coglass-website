Pricing

Everything in Coglass on every plan, priced by the size of your team. Prices exclude VAT. Last updated: October 2026.

All plans include the full system — orders and quoting, surveys, the work bench, production, supplier orders, scheduling, invoicing, the phone app for fitters, the shop-floor iPad app and the customer portal. Plans differ by how many people use Coglass and how much you put through it, not by which features you get.

Billing

Plans are billed monthly in advance by card and roll on month to month — no minimum term and no setup fee. Change plan, update your card or cancel from Manage billing at accounts.coglass.co.uk. See our cancellation and refund policy.

VAT

All prices exclude VAT, which is added at the UK rate (currently 20%).

Anything bigger

Several branches, more than the Business plan allows, or the app under your own name? Email contact@coglass.co.uk and we'll work out what suits.

Prices may change; we give existing customers at least 30 days' notice by email first.
