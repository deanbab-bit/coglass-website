Coglass pricing in the UK

Everything in Coglass on every plan, priced in GBP by the size of your team. Prices exclude VAT. Last updated: October 2026.

All plans include the full system — orders and quoting, surveys, the work bench, production, supplier orders, scheduling, invoicing, the phone app for fitters, the shop-floor iPad app and the customer portal. Plans differ by how many people use Coglass and how much you put through it, not by which features you get.

Billing

Plans are billed monthly in advance in GBP, and you pay by card. They roll on month to month — no minimum term and no setup fee. Change plan, update your card or cancel from Manage billing at accounts.coglass.co.uk. See our cancellation and refund policy.

VAT

All prices exclude VAT, which is added at the UK rate (currently 20%).

Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey and Gibraltar

Businesses in the Crown Dependencies and Gibraltar use the UK site and pay the UK prices in pounds.

WhereVAT on your Coglass subscription
Isle of ManThe Isle of Man is in the UK VAT area, so UK VAT is added at 20% as it is in the UK.
JerseyJersey is outside the UK VAT area, so no UK VAT is added for a Jersey business. Jersey GST is a local tax; check with your accountant whether you need to account for it.
GuernseyGuernsey is outside the UK VAT area and has no VAT or GST, so no UK VAT is added for a Guernsey business.
GibraltarGibraltar is outside the UK VAT area and has no VAT, so no UK VAT is added for a Gibraltar business.

This is how we bill you, not tax advice for your own business.
