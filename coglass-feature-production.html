How it works

From order placed to ready to go — every step tracked

The production board keeps your workshop and your office in sync at all times.

  1. Set up your workshop once

    Answer three short questions and Coglass builds your stations and processes from your How you work settings — what you cut and finish yourself, and what you buy in or send out. Rename, reorder or add steps whenever you like.

  2. Every line gets its own route

    When the job is ordered, each line lands on the board with only the steps it needs: a bought-in pane waits for delivery and a check, a pane you cut goes Cut → Edges & holes → Check & label. A route preview shows which process a product gets, and why.

  3. Tick each step as it's done

    The card says what's next and where. Tick it and the card moves on, skipping lanes it doesn't need. Made a mistake? Undo it straight away, or untick the step later.

  4. Outwork and toughening tracked

    Glass sent to a processor gets Send out and Back in steps, with the days until it's due back. If it's late, the card turns red with a Chase supplier button.

  5. Sealed units pane by pane

    Each pane of a unit you make has its own card. Assemble & seal stays locked until every pane is in, then the unit goes to Check & label.

  6. Ready to go

    When the last step is ticked the card reaches Ready to go. The office is told only when every line of the order is ready, and your automations — a fitting to-do, the collection email — run once.

What's included

Production tools built for glass workshops

Everything your workshop team needs to stay on top of the queue — without spreadsheets or whiteboards.

  • Built from how you workStations and steps start from your In-house / Order in answers. Default processes for bought-in, cut-and-finish and sealed-unit work; copy and edit them, or build your own.
  • A tick for every stepSteps come from the operations on the line — polish, drill, sandblast — plus Cut, Assemble & seal and Check & label. Add your own, such as Wash & inspect or Wrap & rack.
  • Lanes your workshop usesTo plan, To order, Ordered, Cut, Edges & holes, Toughen & laminate, Decorate, Out for processing, Sealed units, Check & label, Check delivery, Ready to go — the board shows only the ones your routes use.
  • Outwork and tougheningSend out and Back in steps with the processor and due date, LATE warnings and a Chase supplier button.
  • Sealed units, pane by paneA card per pane still to come, and sealing locked until every pane is in.
  • Rules for the odd jobSend a customer, customer type, job type or operation down a different process when you need to.
  • Classic viewPrefer simple lanes? Switch to the classic six-lane view in one click. It shares the same ticks, and adds search by order, customer, supplier or product, and needed-by dates from each product's lead time.
  • Shop-floor iPad appWork bench, board and cut list by station on an iPad at the bench, with per-pane ticks and collection sign-off.
  • Barcode labels and scanningBarcode labels for every pane. Scan a works-order barcode to tick the next step, or a glass label to book it in — with the iPad camera or a keyboard-wedge yard scanner.
  • Keeps working offlineThe shop-floor app keeps the board on the iPad and syncs changes when the workshop wifi comes back.
  • Coming soonA station screen that scans the same glass label at every station, and the Coglass desktop app for workshop PCs — station, counter and deliveries-in modes.
