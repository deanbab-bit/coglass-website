How it works

From the supplier's delivery to ready for fitting

Labels and scanning work on any job with glass on it.

  1. Print the labels

    One label per pane, printed from the job on a label roll printer.

  2. Stick them on the glass

    Each label says which job, which opening and which size — "pane 2 of 4, KITCHEN LEFT".

  3. Scan at goods-in

    Scan each label as the delivery comes in — with the shop-floor app's camera, a barcode scanner, or the scan box in the office.

  4. Job ready for fitting

    When the last pane on a job is in, the job moves to glass ready and your "order ready" automations run.

What's included

What goes on the label

A clear, high-contrast design for 100 × 50 mm rolls, with a classic layout too.

  • Job number and customerSo the right glass goes on the right van.
  • Site and openingThe location from the survey in capitals — "FRONT BEDROOM LEFT".
  • Pane n of NKnow at a glance whether the whole job is there.
  • Size and make-upWidth × height in mm, the glass make-up and any processes.
  • BarcodeScanned with the shop-floor app's camera or a barcode scanner.
  • Your company nameA company strip across the top of the label.
  • Receipt by scanScanning a label marks that pane as received against the supplier order.
  • Automations on scanText the customer, add a to-do or notify the office when glass is in.
