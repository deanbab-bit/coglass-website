Glazing jobs across long distances in Namibia

When the next job is 360 km away, the survey has to be right first time and every trip has to count. Here is how to plan for it.

General guidance, not legal or tax advice. Last checked: October 2026.

The distances are the job

Namibian glaziers routinely cover distances that would cross several countries in Europe. A job at the coast is a full day from Windhoek, and a lot of the road between towns is out of mobile coverage.

RouteApproximate road distance
Windhoek – Swakopmundabout 360 km

Windhoek – Lüderitzabout 830–850 km

Approximate distances by the main roads; check your route.

Get the survey right first time

When the site is hours away, a missed measurement or a forgotten photo means another day on the road. Capture everything on the first visit: every opening measured and drawn, photos of each one and of the access, and notes on what the fitting team will need. Then the quote, the glass order and the fitting all work from the same information.

Make each trip count

Group work by area: when a job is ready, look at what else is ready nearby before booking the trip, and fit a survey in on the way. Confirm the day with the customer before the van leaves, so nobody drives four hours to a locked gate.

VAT basics in Namibia

VAT in Namibia is administered by the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA). The standard rate is 15%, and registration is compulsory once your taxable supplies pass N$500,000 in 12 months. Check the detail with NamRA or your accountant.

Where Coglass fits

Coglass is built for work spread over long distances:

  • Surveys on the phone. Each opening is measured, drawn and photographed, with notes, GPS and the access level — and it works with no signal, syncing when you are back in coverage.
  • A planner for spread-out work. Drag surveys, fittings and deliveries onto people and vans, and see which ready jobs are near the day's other stops, scored by driving distance rather than a straight line.
  • Customers know when you are coming. Booking confirmations and on-the-way messages can go out by email, and customers can follow their job on a tracking page on their phone.
  • Xero. Invoices, payments and supplier bills push to your Xero organisation, coded to the accounts and tax rates you choose from your own Xero settings — your returns are then done in Xero.
