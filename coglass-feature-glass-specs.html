How it works

From window on site to glass on the bench

Coglass holds the full make-up of every pane, so the right glass gets cut, sealed and fitted — first time.

  1. Choose the make-up

    Pick single glazed, DGU or TGU and Coglass captures the full build — the glass type for each leaf, cavity, spacer and gas.

  2. Set size & shape

    Width × height in mm, quantity, and any shape — rectangle, arch, circle, oval, gable, trapezoid. Stored to the millimetre — no unit conversion, no product-dropdown guesswork.

  3. Add edgework & processing

    Add edge-finishing and glass-processing operations to any pane — each priced as its own operation alongside the glass.

  4. Draw the features on the pane

    Open the drawing canvas and place holes, cut-outs, Georgian bars, lead and diamond lattices right on the glass — sized off the edges in mm.

  5. Price it automatically

    Coglass prices the glass per m² or per unit from your catalogue, plus every operation — with trade price-lists and quantity breaks applied.

  6. Print the 1:1 template

    Send the lead, Georgian and diamond patterns to a full-size tiled A4 “lay-under” template — the factory lays the glass straight onto it and copies the pattern.

What's included

Glass detail no generic CRM can hold

Every option a real glass order needs — because Coglass was built for glass.

  • Full IGU make-upsDGU and TGU units with the glass type for each leaf, cavity width, spacer bar and gas fill — captured per line, not crammed into one product.
  • Cavity is line-levelSet 12/16/20mm per order line (and a second cavity for triple units) — no exploding your catalogue with a variant of every unit.
  • Edge finishingAdd edge-finishing operations to a pane and price each one individually.
  • Every shapeRectangle, arch, circle, oval, gable, trapezoid, semicircle, triangle and more.
  • Georgian barsBar positions, columns, rows, width and colour — priced per metre of bar and drawn on the pane.
  • Lead & diamondSquare, rectangular and diamond lead lattices with cell size and lead width — drawn and priced.
  • Holes & cut-outsDrilled holes (priced per diameter tier), rectangular cut-outs, letterboxes and corner notches — placed in mm.
  • In-house or bought-in operationsMark any operation as made in-house or bought in from a processor — Coglass raises the outwork order.
  • Save as a productBuild a unit once, save it to your catalogue, and the survey matcher re-uses it automatically next time.
  • 1:1 lay-under templateLead, Georgian and diamond patterns print full-size across A4 with crop marks — the factory copies straight onto the glass.
  • Trade price-listsPer-customer and per-type price lists with quantity breaks and contract windows, applied automatically.
  • RAL paint codesRecord the RAL colour for painted glass and frames against the line.
