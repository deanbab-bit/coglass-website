How a job flows

From the first email to the money in the bank

One record follows the job the whole way — the office, the van and the workshop all see the same thing.

  1. Enquiry

    An email or a call becomes an order — the AI glass scan reads the sizes out of the email for you.

  2. Survey

    Measure, draw and photograph every opening on the phone or iPad, even with no signal.

  3. Quote

    Priced from your catalogue with the glass drawn on each line; the customer accepts online.

  4. Glass ordered

    Purchase orders to your supplier from the job, with the drawing on them — or onto your own production board.

  5. Labels

    A barcode label on every pane; scan it in when the glass arrives and the job shows ready.

  6. Fitting

    Booked on the planner, an on-our-way text to the customer, per-pane sign-off and a signature on site.

  7. Paid

    The invoice is drafted when the fitting is signed off; the customer pays online and it goes to Xero.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which countries is Coglass sold in?

The United Kingdom (including the Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey and Gibraltar), Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Namibia, Botswana and Malta. Each has its own prices in local currency.

Is Coglass built for glass and glazing companies?

Yes. It holds full sealed-unit make-ups (double and triple glazed, cavity, spacer, gas), every edge and shape, and a drawing canvas for holes, Georgian bars, lead and diamond — plus a 1:1 lay-under template the factory copies onto the glass. It is not a general CRM adapted to glass.

Does the phone app work without signal?

Yes. Surveys — measurements, drawings, photos, access notes and GPS — and fitting sign-offs are saved on the phone and sync when you are back in signal. The shop-floor iPad app also keeps working if the workshop wifi drops. The office web app needs an internet connection.

Does it work on iPhone, Android and the web?

Yes — the office uses the web app in a browser, fitters use the iPhone or Android app, and the workshop uses the shop-floor app on an iPad.

Does it work with Xero?

Yes. Invoices, payments and supplier bills push to Xero, coded to the accounts and tax rates you choose from your own Xero organisation.

Who makes Coglass?

Halliday Morrow Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales. Our team is in the UK and supports every country we sell in.
