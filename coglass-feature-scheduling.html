Smart scheduling

Fill the van without ringing round

Most planning time goes on phone calls to find a day that suits, then on driving across town between jobs. These tools cut both.

  • Customers pick the times that suit them

    The problem. You ring to book a fitting, the customer is at work, you leave a message, and they ring back when you are up a ladder.

    How Coglass fixes it. Press Request availability on the order — or let Coglass send it by itself when the job is ready for fitting. The customer gets an email link, taps every arrival window that suits and presses Send my times.

    What you see

    • A “When are you free?” page with your logo and the arrival windows you choose, such as 8–10 and 9–11
    • A notification when they reply, and their times on the order
    • Jobs still waiting for a reply listed on their own, so you know who to phone

  • Suggested runs

    The problem. Ready jobs are spread across the area, and working out which ones go together on which day takes an evening with a map.

    How Coglass fixes it. Suggested runs looks at every survey and fitting not yet booked, groups the jobs that are close together, and picks the best day for each group from the times customers said suit them.

    What you see

    • Runs by area for the next 7, 14 or 30 days, with the total hours and how many customers are free that day
    • A week view, and a grid of every job against every day with a tick where the customer is free
    • Schedule this run: pick the fitter, check the date, and the jobs go on the planner

  • Nearby jobs

    The problem. A fitter has a job across town in the morning and nothing after — and there is probably another ready job five minutes away that nobody thought of.

    How Coglass fixes it. Drop a job on someone’s day, or press the pin on their row, and Coglass lists other ready jobs only a short drive out of their way. It uses real driving time between the stops already booked, not distance on a map.

    What you see

    • Each job with the extra driving it adds, such as “+8 min”, grouped as Close, Short detour or Further
    • Surveys, fittings and deliveries all offered
    • Tick the ones you want and add them to the day — they go into the first free gaps, and nothing already booked moves

  • Anytime customers

    The problem. Schools, surgeries, offices and trade counters are open all day. Asking them to pick a time just slows the booking down.

    How Coglass fixes it. Switch on Flexible scheduling for the customer. Coglass stops sending them the availability email and treats them as free 8am to 6pm every day, so their jobs fit into any run.

    What you see

    • An Anytime label on their jobs in Nearby jobs, whatever day you are planning
    • An override on a single order when one site is different

  • Glass that won’t be in on time

    The problem. A fitting is booked for Tuesday, and on Monday you find the glass isn’t due until Thursday.

    How Coglass fixes it. The planner checks each booking against the job’s purchase orders.

    What you see

    • The booking card turns hatched red when its glass won’t arrive in time
    • Chase PO from the booking’s job card
How it works

Your whole team's week, planned in minutes

The Coglass planning board gives managers a full view of who is doing what, when — and pushes the schedule straight to everyone's phones.

  1. Open the planning board

    The scheduling view shows your full team in rows and upcoming dates in columns. At a glance you can see who's free, who's booked, and which days have gaps — for the week or the next two weeks.

  2. Assign jobs to team members

    Drag an order onto a team member's slot for a specific date. Set the visit type (survey, fitting, delivery), start time and duration. The assignment appears in that person's mobile app immediately — no call needed.

  3. Allocate vehicles

    Put a van with each team member for each day from the same board.

  4. Block absences

    Record holidays, sick days and training in the absence calendar. Absent team members show as blocked on the planning board — you can't accidentally book them in on a day they're off.

  5. Team see their schedule on their phone

    Bookings show in the field team's app — customer, address, visit type and time — without a call from the office.

  6. See who’s on site

    The Map view shows the day’s stops and where clocked-in staff are. When a fitter arrives or leaves, the booking shows how long they have been on site — and Print all gives every team its run sheet for the day.
