How it works

Captured once, used everywhere

The survey, the work bench and the sign-off on site feed every document — nobody types it twice.

  1. Survey

    The access level recorded on the survey starts the equipment list on a new risk assessment in the phone app.

  2. Quote and order

    Branded quote PDFs with the glass drawn on each line; purchase orders to your supplier with the same drawing.

  3. Collection or delivery

    The customer signs on screen; the signed note is stored on the job.

  4. Invoice

    A branded invoice PDF, emailed in your layout, with a link to pay online.

What's included

Documents Coglass produces for you

All in your logo and colours, all kept on the job.

  • RAMS from the surveyScaffold, ladders, harness: the access level on the survey fills the equipment list on a new RAMS in the phone app.
  • RAMS PDFRisk assessment and method statement as a branded PDF, kept on the job.
  • Signed collection notesItemised, signed on screen with the collector's name, and emailed to the customer.
  • Signed delivery notesWith the customer's signature and a proof photo, ready to download.
  • Branded quotesYour logo and colour, the glass drawn on each line, and a link to accept online.
  • Branded invoicesDeposit, proforma and final invoices with totals, balance and a pay-online link.
  • Purchase orders with drawingsNumbered per job, with the pane drawn on the PO, emailed to the supplier with the PDF attached.
  • Cutting sheetsA cutting-sheet PDF for the workshop, grouped by station.
  • Branded emailsEvery customer email uses one layout with your logo — and you can write your own templates.
