Coglass pricing in Malta

Everything in Coglass on every plan, priced in EUR by the size of your team. Prices exclude VAT. Last updated: October 2026.

All plans include the full system — orders and quoting, surveys, the work bench, production, supplier orders, scheduling, invoicing, the phone app for fitters, the shop-floor iPad app and the customer portal. Plans differ by how many people use Coglass and how much you put through it, not by which features you get.

Billing

Plans are billed monthly in advance in EUR, and you pay by card. They roll on month to month — no minimum term and no setup fee. Change plan, update your card or cancel from Manage billing at accounts.coglass.co.uk. See our cancellation and refund policy.

VAT

Prices exclude VAT. Coglass is sold to businesses by Halliday Morrow Ltd, a UK company.

If your business has a valid Maltese VAT number, give it to us when you sign up: no VAT is added to our invoice and you account for it yourself under the reverse charge.

No VAT number? You can still sign up: Maltese VAT at 18% is added at checkout and shown on your invoice.

Support

Our team is in the UK, one hour behind Malta. Email or call during business hours and we usually reply within one working day.

Anything bigger

Several branches, more than the Business plan allows, or the app under your own name? Email contact@coglass.co.uk and we'll work out what suits.
