How it works

From an enquiry to a priced order in a few clicks

The scan works on the message text in your Coglass inbox and on the messages kept against a job.

  1. Open the enquiry

    A customer email lands in the Coglass inbox, or a message arrives on a job from the chat on your quote or tracking page.

  2. Scan it for glass

    Press Scan. The AI picks out each pane — quantity, width, height and the type of glass described.

  3. Check the matches

    Each pane is matched to a product in your catalogue and shown with its selling price. Anything it can't match waits for you to pick the product.

  4. Add to an order

    Create a new order from the message, or add the lines to one you already have. Several jobs in one message can be grouped separately.

What's included

Built for the way glass enquiries arrive

Plain-English emails with sizes in any order — the scan does the reading, you stay in charge of what goes on the job.

  • Reads the message textQuantities, widths, heights and glass descriptions picked out of the email or message text.
  • Matched to your catalogueEach pane is matched to one of your own products, priced from your catalogue.
  • You check before it's addedNothing goes on an order until you've looked at the lines; unmatched panes wait for you.
